What this site collects

Nothing at all until you answer the cookie question. That is not a policy — it is how the page is built: every measurement signal starts denied in the document head, before any script from Google is allowed to run.

If you refuse

Nothing is stored on your device and nothing identifying is sent. The visit still registers as one anonymous, unattributable hit — Google calls this consent mode, and it is the reason refusing here removes you from the data rather than removing you from the count.

You will not be asked again on this browser. That preference is kept in your browser's own storage and never leaves it.

If you accept

Two Google services, and only these two.

Google Analytics

How many people came, which pages they read, roughly where they were, and what brought them. Aggregate figures, not a profile with your name on it.

Google Ads

Whether a visit came from an advertisement, so money spent on one can be judged. If you later subscribe to anything here, your address is also used to match that signup — Google calls this enhanced conversions, and it happens only with this consent.

What is not here, and will not be

  • No other analytics. No heat maps, no session recorders, nothing that replays what you did on the page.
  • No social buttons. The links to GitHub and LinkedIn are ordinary links; those companies learn nothing until you click one.
  • No advertising networks beyond Google Ads, and nothing sold or handed to anybody.
  • No fonts from a third party. They are served from this site, so reading a page does not announce you to a font provider.

Every tracker on a site is a third party given your readers for free. Two is already a decision; twelve is a habit.

Changing your mind

Clear this site's data in your browser and the question comes back on your next visit.
