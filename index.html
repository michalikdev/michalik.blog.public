Data engineering, local AI, and the tools in between

Eight years building data systems, six of them at Google. These days I build for companies of my own — document pipelines, text recognition, language models on hardware I can touch. This is where the work gets written down: what the problem was, what it cost, and what broke on the way.

Most of what is here is about getting data out of documents and into something usable: text recognition on scanned records, language models run locally rather than through an API, pipelines that hold up when an archive is slow, and the plain automation underneath all of it. Some of it is about the parts that were not worth it.
