Paweł Michalik

Data engineer · co-founder of Lex&Co and Polish Descent, Kraków

I co-founded two companies in Kraków: a legal practice, and a service helping people of Polish ancestry claim Polish citizenship — tracing a family line, finding and registering the records, and carrying a case through to a passport. Before that I spent eight years as a data analyst and data engineer, six of them working at Google. I still build: most of what these two companies run on is software I wrote.

What I work on

Data engineering
Eight years of it before the companies: warehouses, the pipelines that fill them, and query performance at terabyte scale — at Google on BigQuery, and before that on operational data in banking.
Distributed infrastructure
Deploying across machines rather than onto one. At Google that meant their platform; now it means infrastructure of my own, running across two sites.
Local AI
Language models and text recognition on hardware I own, used to speed up work that is not allowed to leave the office.
Automation
Taking repeated office work — intake, filing, chasing archives — and making it run without being watched.
Microservices
Splitting one large program into small ones that can be understood, deployed and replaced separately. Including the part where that costs more before it pays.
Marketing and reach
The other half of running a company: building the network of information that puts the right answer in front of the person searching for it.
